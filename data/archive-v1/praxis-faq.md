# Zahnarztpraxis Seefeld (FICTIONAL DEMO PRACTICE — not a real business)

Address: Musterstrasse 12, 8008 Zürich (fictional). Phone: 044 000 00 00 (fictional). Email: info@praxis-seefeld.example

## Opening hours
- Monday–Thursday: 08:00–12:00 and 13:30–18:00
- Friday: 08:00–14:00
- Saturday/Sunday: closed
- Public holidays (Zurich): closed

## Emergencies
- During opening hours: call the practice, emergency slots are kept free every day at 11:30 and 17:30.
- Outside opening hours: Zahnärztlicher Notfalldienst Zürich, phone 044 000 00 01 (fictional number for demo).

## Services
- Check-up & dental hygiene (Dentalhygiene): 60 min, CHF 160–220 depending on effort.
- Fillings, crowns, root canal treatment.
- Teeth whitening (Bleaching): from CHF 450.
- Implants: consultation required; quote after examination.
- Children's dentistry: from age 3. School dental service (Schulzahnklinik) vouchers accepted.
- Invisalign / aligners: free initial consultation (30 min).

## Appointments
- Book online via the booking link on the website, by phone, or by email.
- New patients: please bring ID, insurance card (if you have supplementary dental insurance) and any recent X-rays.
- Cancellations: free up to 24 hours before the appointment. Later cancellations or no-shows are charged CHF 80.

## Payment & insurance
- Basic health insurance (KVG/Grundversicherung) does NOT cover routine dental care in Switzerland. Supplementary dental insurance may cover part of it.
- Payment by invoice (30 days), TWINT, debit card or credit card.
- Cost estimates (Kostenvoranschlag) are provided for treatments over CHF 500.

## Languages
German, English, Italian.

## Parking & access
- 2 visitor parking spaces behind the building; tram 2/4 stop "Feldeggstrasse" 3 minutes on foot.
- Wheelchair accessible (lift).

## Rules for the assistant
- Never give a medical diagnosis. For pain, swelling or bleeding: recommend calling the practice or the emergency service.
- Never collect health details in chat. For appointments, point to the booking link or phone number.
