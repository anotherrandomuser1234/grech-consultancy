- The tax return is due on 31 March of the following year.

## Documents needed for a private tax return
- Salary statement(s) (Lohnausweis)
- Bank and securities account statements as of 31 December
- Pillar 3a certificate (Säule 3a), pension fund buy-in confirmations (Pensionskasse)
- Health insurance premium statement and invoices for medical costs not covered
- Donation receipts, childcare costs
- Mortgage/debt interest statements, property maintenance costs (if property owner)
- Last year's tax assessment (Veranlagung)
